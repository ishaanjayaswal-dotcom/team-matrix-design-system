Design System

How Team Matrix looks and sounds. One logo, five colours, two fonts, one gradient, and the parts to build anything from the website to the pit banner.

Open on GitHub Use the tokens
Identity

Logo

White lettering with circuit lines. It is always white and always on dark purple. Use the transparent version so it sits on any of our backgrounds.

On main · #0E0C14
On second · #513899
On gradient

Do

  • Use team-matrix-logo-white-trimmed.png by default.
  • Leave space around it at least the height of the word "TEAM".
  • Keep it at least 120px wide on screen and 30mm in print.
  • Use the original Canva or vector file for banners, jerseys and the robot. These files are 262 × 138 px.

Don't

  • Recolour it, stretch it, rotate it or add shadows.
  • Put it on white or light backgrounds.
  • Shorten the name to "Matrix". It is always Team Matrix.
  • Change official FIRST or FTC logos. Download them from firstinspires.org.
Colour

Five team colours

Very dark purple underneath, a bold violet second background, white headings, light purple text and one violet accent. No green anywhere. Tap a colour to copy its hex code.

Helpers

Worked out from the team colours to fix readability. Use them only where the note says.

Readability check

TextOnSampleContrastResult

WCAG 2.2: 4.5 or more for normal text, 3 or more for large text, buttons and icons.

Gradient

The glow

A violet glow top left that fades into logo indigo. Put text on the darker right or bottom part. White on the bright spot is only 3.1:1.

Text goes here

On the darker side
--tm-gradient-brand: radial-gradient(80% 90% at 17% 27%, #9E7CFF 0%, #3B2585 45%, #1A0A41 100%);
Typography

Two fonts

Bebas Neue for every heading: tall, condensed, capitals only, one weight. Poppins for everything you read. Both are free on Google Fonts and in Canva.

Headings
AA 20870

Bebas Neue 400. Capitals only. Never bold.

Body
Aa

Poppins 400, 500, 600, 700.

Space and shape

4px grid

Every gap and margin is a step on the 4px grid. Corners are gently rounded: 6px small parts, 12px buttons and cards, 20px big panels.

Voice

How we sound

Students who build serious machines. Short sentences, real numbers, and credit given by name.

Lead with the robot

"Our turret aims without turning the drivetrain."

Numbers beat adjectives

"Ranked #1 of 46 at the India Championship." Not "one of the best teams".

Thank people by name

Sponsors, mentors and alliance partners, every time.
