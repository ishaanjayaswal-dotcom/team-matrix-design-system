Identity

The logo

The mark is a 5 × 5 dot matrix with the letter M switched on. One cell, the vertex of the M, burns aqua. That is the team: a grid of people, one signal at the centre. The wordmark is set in Unbounded and locked to the same grid.

Construction and clear space

  • ✓Keep one pitch (20 units at logo scale) of empty space around the mark.
  • ✓Use the full lockup when space allows. Use the mark alone on the robot, avatars and small sizes.
  • ✓Minimum size: mark 24px tall on screen, 8mm in print.
  • ✕Do not recolour the hot cell, rotate the mark or switch on extra cells.
  • ✕Do not set the wordmark in any font other than the supplied outlines.
  • ✕Do not put the colour mark on busy photos. Use mono white with a dark scrim instead.
Colour

Midnight and Paper

Two worlds, taken straight from our sponsorship deck. Midnight is the default: the arena with the lights down. Paper is for print, reports and anything read in daylight. Violet is the lit cell, aqua is the signal, sand is for honours. Tap any colour to copy its hex.

Red and blue are reserved for the two FTC alliances. Never use them as brand colours.

Typography

Three voices

Unbounded is wide and engineered. Use it for headlines, scores and anything that needs to be read from the stands. Instrument Sans carries every sentence. JetBrains Mono is for team numbers, labels, timers and code.

Scale

Grid and shape

Measured in cells

The base unit is the matrix cell: 16px with a 4px gap, so one pitch is 20px. Every space, margin and radius comes from that. If a layout looks off, count the cells.

Chips and tags use the cell radius so they echo the logo. Cards use md. Only large image plates use lg. We do not use pills.

Use it

Plug it in
